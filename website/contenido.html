Para empezar

Un recorrido más largo

Chiltern v4

Banbury General → Bicester North → Princes Risborough → High Wycombe. Cuatro paradas y 62,68 km.

Elegí la actividad RS_Football Special y la formación Birmingham Pullman para repetir el viaje que completamos. La formación de vapor RS_Football Special también permite probar la cabina Hall y la grúa de agua de Banbury.

Se comprobaron arranque, marcha por inercia, frenado, reservas y sonido original de esas dos formaciones y de 1960CentralWR8Car. El eléctrico se probó con un perfil separado de cuarto riel a 630 V; esta prueba no asegura que todas las vías de la ruta tengan alimentación compatible.

The historic Burrinjuck railway, built in 1907 and decommissioned in 1929, was a narrow gauge route between Goondah and Burrinjuck in New South Wales, Australia. Craven Timber Railway

Autor: Peter Newell

El catálogo no indica el tamaño

Descripción del autor

The Craven Timber railway is a 5.8 miles / 9.6 km branch of the North Coast line located in New South Wales, Australia. This typical timber 'tramway' was used to transport timber out of The Glen to a sawmill located adjacent to Craven Station (180 miles north of Sydney). Built to light railway standards with a number of tight 120m curves the line was operated by two saddle tank steam locomotives, PWD32 and 529X. It crossed the Wards River six times as it wound up the picturesque river valley.

Manning River Breakwall Railway

Autor: Peter Newell

Descarga: 71 MiB

Descripción del autor

The Manning River Breakwater railway was built in 1895 to transport rock from a quarry at Crowdy Head to the entrance of the river at Harrington. Powered by a Baldwin Steam Tram motor, approximately 600,000 tons of rock was transported over time from the quarry to Harrington. Operation of this route was relatively simple as it was fairly flat with a single locomotive, so that signalling was not required.

Montana Rail Link - Mullan Pass

Autor: TrainSimulations.net

El catálogo no indica el tamaño

Descripción del autor

Originally built by the Northern Pacific in the 1880's, Mullan Pass is one of the most dramatic Rocky Mountain crossings in the United States, with a ruling westbound grade of 2.2%. The line came under control of the Burlington Northern in the early 1970's, and was leased to Montana Rail Link (MRL) in the late 1980's. While MRL maintains the right of way today, the line is owned by the BNSF railway, and is used extensively to move BNSF tonnage trains to the west coast. BNSF traffic is primarily Powder River coal and grain from the Midwest. BNSF also moves manifest, intermodal, and Boeing aircraft part trains. The remaining traffic is MRL-specific manifest trains. This is a modern interpretation of this piece of railroad, complete with high horsepower AC traction locomotives. For those with a passion for nostalgia, MRL still uses cabooses on some local freights.

Southern Pacific - Shasta Route

Autor: TrainSimulations.net

El catálogo no indica el tamaño

Descripción del autor

Southern Pacific's picturesque Shasta Route was the main artery of SP traffic north of Roseville California to the Pacific Northwest. For decades, Mount Shasta created the perfect marketing piece for SP's passenger services, and even today, countless photos of Amtrak's Coast Starlight include the imposing mountain in the background. TrainSimulations Shasta Route is a mid-1990's representation of SP's final years in the Pacific Northwest before their merger with Union Pacific. This single track mainline hosted countless manifest trains carrying everything from California produce to forest products from the Pacific Northwest, as well as numerous intermodal trains. The steep climb north from Dunsmuir, as well as tight curves makes for challenging mainline operations. A large amount of SP power and rolling stock are included, and for the passenger train enthusiasts, a mid-90s Amtrak Coast Starlight consist is also included.

¿Dónde queda lo que descargás?

En la carpeta de datos de tu usuario. Snap usa su carpeta común para conservarlo entre actualizaciones. En la biblioteca, Abrir carpeta te lleva a la copia que estás usando.

Cuando aparece una edición nueva se guarda junto a la anterior, con la fecha del origen si existe y un identificador de la copia. Así podés volver a la versión que ya tenías.

Si faltan archivos, Copiar diagnóstico reúne los nombres y sus destinos. Buscalos en el repositorio original del contenido y, después de agregarlos, pulsá Reauditar esta formación. Las descargas no forman parte del código del proyecto.
